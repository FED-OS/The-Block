Quick answers

No silly questions. Here is the short version.

Is The Block for beginners?

Yes. The site uses plain language and encourages small first steps. You can skip anything that is not useful to you.

Do I need to pay to use the resources?

No. This starter site presents the resources as free. If you later add paid options, explain exactly what people receive and what they will pay.

What should I do first?

Choose one of the three starting points at the top of the page. If you are unsure, use the next-step planner and focus on the action that takes 30 minutes or less.

How does the forum work?

Pick a room that fits your topic, start a thread or reply to one, and keep the energy useful. You can attach images to threads and replies. The forum is live on Supabase — sign in with your email (magic link) to post, and your threads, replies, and pics are shared with the whole crew. Need a local-only version? Ask about demo mode.

What is Black Leaders Management?

A coding, AI, and tech development & management agency built for Black talent. Developers and PMs get training, mentorship, contracts, and placements. Companies get matched with vetted talent. Reach the team at blm@fedpromptly.com.
